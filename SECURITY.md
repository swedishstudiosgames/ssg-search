# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to establish a framework for protecting SwedishStudios Games's information assets from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction. This policy ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our data and systems.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff, and other workers at SwedishStudios Games, including all personnel affiliated with third parties who are granted access to SwedishStudios Games's networks, systems, or data.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
* **Management:** Responsible for providing necessary resources to support information security and ensuring compliance with this policy.
* **IT/Security Department:** Responsible for implementing, monitoring, and maintaining technical security controls, as well as providing security training.
* **Employees/Users:** Responsible for understanding and adhering to this policy, protecting their credentials, and reporting any suspicious activities.

## 4. Acceptable Use of Assets
* Company-provided devices and network resources are primarily for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with productivity or compromise security.
* Users must not use company assets to access, store, or distribute illegal, offensive, or malicious content.
* Installing unauthorized software on company devices is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Access Control and Authentication
* **Principle of Least Privilege:** Users will only be granted the minimum access rights necessary to perform their job duties.
* **Passwords:** All users must use strong, unique passwords for their accounts. Passwords must be at least 64 characters long and should not be shared.
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is required for all access to SwedishStudios Games’s network, email, and critical business applications.
* **Termination:** Access rights must be promptly revoked upon the termination of an employee or contractor's relationship with the company.

## 6. Data Protection and Confidentiality
* **Data Classification:** All company data should be classified based on its sensitivity (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted).
* **Handling Sensitive Data:** Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted when stored and transmitted. It must not be shared with unauthorized parties or stored on unapproved personal devices.
* **Physical Security:** Desktops, laptops, and physical documents must be secured when left unattended (e.g., lock screens, clear desk policy).

## 7. Incident Reporting
* All users are required to immediately report any suspected or actual security incidents (e.g., lost devices, suspected phishing emails, unauthorized access) to the IT/Security Department at contact@swedishstudiosgames.com.
* Users must not attempt to investigate or remediate security incidents themselves unless explicitly authorized.

## 8. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and potential legal action depending on the severity of the breach.

## 9. Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes to the organization's environment occur, to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness.
